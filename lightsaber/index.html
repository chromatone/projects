MIDI Light Saber
We are building an ESP32 powered RGB LED lightsaber, that is going far beyond fighting and bouncing shots back to stormtroopers. We are creating a completely new Visual Music Instrument, that we can use as a platform to explore the whole new space of intermedia art and science.
